3. ATTRIBUTION METHOD AND RATIONALE
   LAYER C: exact linear-model logit decomposition of the classifier (LogisticRegression is
   linear, so coef*value is an EXACT per-dimension contribution - no approximation needed).

   closed-form input attribution; occlusion was chosen over SHAP/gradients for its simplicity
   and lack of baseline-choice ambiguity beyond the (documented) TRAIN-mean reference vector.
   These are two DIFFERENT quantities and are never conflated or added together.

   - Evaluated on VALIDATION only (TEST has zero Infiltration samples, per Feature 7-9).
   - No LLM provider was configured in this run; the LLM layer's grounding logic was
     exercised on the prompt construction, but no live natural-language text was generated.
