  matching the documented FIRST window's end (10:55) to the second.
  Together these two independent, exact matches prove the CSV's
  Timestamp column, the official attack-schedule table, and real packet
  timestamps ALL share the same clock: UTC, with NO offset. This
  resolves Step 2's timezone question conclusively (not invented,
  empirically derived from two independent exact-second matches).

  What this candidate (part2) captures is the SECOND attack session,

  provenance finding - just not one usable for direct flow-vs-CSV-row
